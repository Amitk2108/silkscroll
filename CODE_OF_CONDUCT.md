# Code of Conduct

SilkScroll is a small, friendly project. We want everyone who uses or contributes to it to feel welcome,
whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Accept decisions gracefully, and keep discussions on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and to anyone representing
the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact details on the repository owner's GitHub
profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may warn, temporarily
ban or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge harmful to the community.

This code of conduct is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
